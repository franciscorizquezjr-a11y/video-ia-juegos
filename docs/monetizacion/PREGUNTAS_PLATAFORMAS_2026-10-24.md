# Preguntas para las plataformas — 24/10/2026

Borradores para copiar en sus formularios públicos de contacto o soporte. No enviados. No aceptar una campaña para poder consultar sus condiciones. Las respuestas deben identificar programa, canales autorizados y versión contractual aplicable.

## Presentación común, en inglés

Hello. I am a Spain-based creator behind two gaming discovery brands, Next Loot Games and Party Loot. I publish short, faceless videos with original commentary and editing, using authorized publisher footage. My workflow can include AI-assisted scripting and synthetic narration. Before registering or accepting paid work, I would appreciate written clarification of the following points.

## Keymailer

Referencia: [condiciones](https://keymailer.co/terms-and-conditions-gamer-access/) y [contacto público](https://keymailer.co/contact/).

1. May I manage both brands, including two separate YouTube channels, through ONE creator account? Which channels/platforms can that account link?
2. May I use those channels with Trapster, Creators.gg, Wehype, Dare Drop and Lurkit for separate campaigns? Please clarify how clauses 8.2.2.4 and 4.12 apply to a creator who does not operate a competing platform.
3. Does my described publisher-footage/TTS workflow comply with clause 8.4.16 when the publisher expressly authorizes the material?
4. Is the Hexplorando paid short-form campaign still accepting applications? Please confirm eligibility, whether synthetic narration is accepted, agreed fee, payment deadline, payer, view requirements, revisions and permitted platforms.
5. What happens to the content license when the account is deleted? Please clarify clauses 6.4 and 4.10.

No pedir dos cuentas como solución automática. Si admiten varias, conservar su autorización escrita y su alcance.

## Wehype

Referencia: [información para creadores](https://www.wehype.com/for-creators), contacto oficial contact@wehype.com.

1. Can one owner manage two gaming brands and their two YouTube channels under one creator account? Can those channels simultaneously use other sponsorship platforms for unrelated campaigns?
2. Do you currently offer paid YouTube Shorts campaigns for faceless, publisher-footage videos with synthetic narration? What channel metrics and country requirements apply?
3. What is the current minimum withdrawal? How would a final balance below that amount be paid if I close the account or receive no further offers?
4. Please confirm payment method, currency, all withdrawal/FX fees for Spain and ordinary/express payment times.
5. Which networks and brands may publish each commissioned video? What are the exact paid-media/Spark Ads permissions, duration and compensation? May the creator reuse the work elsewhere?
6. Which YouTube/TikTok permissions and private metrics are required, and what remains stored after disconnecting a channel or deleting the account?

Antes de una oferta, pedir también su contrato particular, política de cancelación y entregables; no dar por autorizada la publicación duplicada en las cinco cuentas.

3. Please provide the conversion rate, withdrawal minimum, fees, expiry rules, payout method, deadline and budget-reservation rules applicable to that cash program.
4. Can one owner manage two gaming brands and keep the same channels connected to other sponsorship platforms?
5. Does enabled Clip & Earn footage permit narration, substantial editing, cross-platform publication and any sublicensing required by your agreement? What restrictions apply to music?

## Cómo conservar las respuestas

Guardar fecha, remitente/dominio y texto íntegro de la autorización recibida; asociarla al programa y campaña. Una respuesta comercial genérica no debe tomarse como modificación del contrato. Si hay excepción, pedir confirmación expresa de la cláusula y del caso autorizado. No enviar contraseñas ni documentos de identidad por un canal de soporte que no haya solicitado formalmente una verificación segura.
