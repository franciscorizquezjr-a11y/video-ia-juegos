## Your Task

Create a precise, frame-accurate editing plan that:

1. **Original source video duration** is exactly {source_duration} seconds. Every clip 'start' and 'end' timestamp MUST be strictly between 0.0 and {source_duration} seconds.
2. **Total output video duration** must be exactly {target_duration} seconds (audio_duration + {audio_tail_padding}s padding).
3. The voiceover starts at 0.0 seconds.
4. The final {audio_tail_padding} seconds contain only visuals (ending shot, logo, or fade-out) with no voiceover.
5. Clips are selected from the ORIGINAL source video using timestamps from that video.
6. The sum of all clip durations must equal the total output video duration ({target_duration} seconds).
7. Clip order follows the narrative arc from your storyboard.

## Editing Rules

- Each clip must have a clear start and end time (in seconds) referencing the SOURCE video
- Clips can be from ANY part of the source video — you are reordering them for maximum impact
- CRITICAL: Do NOT select video segments containing large on-screen text, permanent subtitles, watermarks, or logos. Cropping to a vertical 9:16 layout will cut off these elements, looking unprofessional. Focus on clean, text-free gameplay action.
- Keep clips between 1.5 and 8 seconds each
- The hook clip (first clip) should be 2-4 seconds
- Avoid clips shorter than 1 second (they feel jarring)
- Ensure clips don't overlap in the source video when possible
- Order clips to match the emotional progression from your storyboard

## What You Must NOT Do

- Do NOT generate FFmpeg commands
- Do NOT suggest effects, filters, or transitions
- Do NOT modify the voiceover script
- Focus ONLY on clip selection and timing
